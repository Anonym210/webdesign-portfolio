Who is behind this

I am Ejdin Aliti, and I build your site myself.

No team, no agency, nothing handed off to third parties. You tell me what your business does in our first conversation — and the same person delivers the design, the build, the launch and every change afterwards.

I work out of Dübendorf and mainly look after businesses in the greater Zurich area and the rest of German-speaking Switzerland. I write every page by hand instead of clicking it together in a website builder — which is why my sites load fast, work on any device and contain nothing you do not need.

Why local businesses? Because that is where the difference is greatest. A tradesperson, a restaurant or a practice gets noticeably more calls from a good website — at a large corporation the same work disappears into the noise. And because I would rather talk to people who know their craft than to three departments at once.

  • You always talk to me. No ticket system, no hold music, no changing contacts.
  • I explain what I am doing. No jargon you have to look up — you should understand what you are paying for.
  • A reply within one working day. Enquiries received before 6 pm are answered by the next working day at the latest.
  • Hand-written code. No website builder, no WordPress bloat, no forty plugins that need maintaining.

Exactly what is included — and what is not

Included in every package

  • Design and build — bespoke, not a purchased template.
  • Domain, hosting and SSL setup — the contracts are in your name.
  • One email address on your domain, set up and tested.
  • Legal notice and privacy policy — I insert vetted templates and fill them with your details. That is craft, not legal advice; the legal review remains your responsibility.
  • Image editing — cropping, brightening, resizing for the web.
  • Handover of every login and file after launch.
  • 30 days of corrections for anything noticed after launch that was part of the quote.

Not included — and what it costs

  • Domain and hosting themselves: roughly CHF 8–20 a month depending on the provider. You pay the provider directly; I add no markup.
  • External services (booking, shop, newsletter): you pay those fees directly to the provider concerned.
  • Photographer or paid stock images: charged by the hour. Royalty-free images I select at no cost.
  • Language switcher for Start and Business: from CHF 390.– per additional language. The base language (German, English or French) is already included in every package at no extra charge. For the switcher I arrange and check the translation and pass the cost on at cost price — or you supply the text yourself, in which case this item does not apply.
  • Changes after launch without a support package: CHF 90.– per hour started, agreed in advance.

Payment and support

  • No deposit. You pay only once the site is live and you are satisfied — one invoice, payable within 30 days.
  • Walking away after the first draft: Call it off if you do not like the direction. No invoice follows.
  • Support package from CHF 29.– a month: hosting monitoring, backups, updates and up to 30 minutes of changes a month — swapping texts, prices, opening hours or images.
  • After the first year the support package continues monthly and can be cancelled at any month's end. The price stays the same as long as the scope does.
  • Without a support package there are no ongoing costs on my side. The site keeps running; you get in touch only when something comes up.

I am not liable for VAT — the amounts shown are final prices.
